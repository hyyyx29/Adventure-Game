Welcome to the game of Adventure.

This game is called "Collosal Cave Adventure".  The story was originally
written in 1976 by developer Will Crowther.

To play, a player moves between locations and interacts with objects by
typing one or two word commands.

The game is based on Crowther's experiences caving in Mammoth Cave in
Kentucky and it was intended to be accessible to his two daughters.

You can consider extending or adding to this game for your final project.
You can add sounds to it to make it more accessible, for example.
You may want to add new features, like dragons or spells.
